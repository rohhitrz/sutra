# Questions Travellers Ask

Posted by the Lamplighters' Guild at every waystone, in the Guild's plain style. Answers are short
on purpose: they are read standing up, in wind.

---

**How many waystones are there?**

Forty. One at Halvern Reach, forty at Kesh Ithar.

---

**Who built them?**

Nobody knows. No record of the Line is older than the Concord of 1012 and the stones were there
first. Anyone who tells you otherwise is selling you something.

---

**How do I know a route is open?**

Look at the fire-pan. If the fires are lit at both ends of a route, the route is open. If they are
out, it is shut, and no licensed caravan may use it. Only the Guild lights them.

---

**What is the Waystone Tithe?**

A levy of the Harbour Syndicate, charged per wagon for each waystone your wagon passes, counting the
first and the last. The rate and the stretch it applies to have changed; ask at the tithe office for
the schedule in force for your passage, and do not take an old figure from another traveller.

---

**Where is the tithe counted?**

Beneath the first waystone, in the Syndic's courtyard at Halvern Reach. The count taken there is the
claim on your caravan for the whole season.

---

**Can I cross the Vessel River anywhere but Varro's Ford?**

Not with a loaded wagon. The shelf at the ninth waystone is the only crossing in the river's length.
Unloaded, on foot, in a dry season, people manage it elsewhere and some of them come back.

---

**Is the Vessel always crossable at the Ford?**

No. Roughly four months in eight it is not, and which four varies by the year. Ask at the Ford.

---

**What is the water toll?**

The Ashen Compact holds the cisterns in the Flats, between the twelfth and twenty-fourth waystones,
and charges for them. The rate is set each season. It is not negotiable at the cistern's edge, and
arguing about it there has gone badly for people.

---

**Is the Compact dangerous?**

The Compact announces its rate in advance and keeps to it. Draw your own conclusions, and carry
water anyway.

---

**How much water should I carry across the Flats?**

Enough to cross the Flats. The Guild does not give a figure, because the figure depends on your
wagons, your season and your beasts, and travellers who are given a number stop thinking.

---

**What is at Ossuary Gate?**

The seventeenth waystone, under a broken arch. A cistern, a wind-break, a fire-pan, and a tender.
It is the midpoint of the Line by distance and by water.

---

**Who is the tender at Ossuary Gate?**

Hesh, who has no voice and keeps the Gate's records in knotted cord. Hesh will give you water. Do
not shout; it does not help and he has heard it before.

---

**Why cord and not a ledger?**

Because the cords go back thirty-one seasons and have never been found wrong, and nobody has yet
explained what a ledger would improve.

---

**Is the Thennic Spur open?**

Check the fires at the fourteenth and twenty-second waystones. The pass was shut by a rockfall and
has since been cleared and relit; a traveller reading this at an unknown date should trust the
fire-pans and the Guild's notice over this sheet, which is reposted only when the Guild gets to it.

---

**What does the Spur save me?**

About twelve days and eight waystones of the Flats. It costs you the cistern and the cord at
Ossuary Gate, which on a bad passage are the two things that keep you alive.

---

**What is sold at the Glass Bazaar?**

Glass above everything. Then salt, brass and carriage.

---

**Is salt worth carrying north?**

Salt moves in both directions and the margin is thin and seasonal. The Guild does not advise on
trade. The merchant houses at Kesh Ithar keep ledgers of what salt actually fetched by the season,
and a figure from a ledger beats a figure from a sheet nailed to a post.

---

**What is the Tide Clock?**

A water clock at Halvern Reach with four dials. Three of them are explained on the hall's own
notice. The fourth is behind a brass plate and the Guild has nothing to tell you about it.

---

**Can I see the fourth dial?**

No.

---

**When can I walk on Old Mirrow?**

In Deepwinter, in most years, when the water at Mirrowmere drops. It dropped out of season once in
recent record. Do not plan a passage around it.

---

**How far is Mirrowmere from the Line?**

The Guild does not post a distance. The tracks are seasonal, none has been surveyed, and the figures
travellers give differ wildly enough that posting one would get somebody killed.

---

**Who do I complain to?**

About the fires, the Guild. About the tithe, the Harbour Syndicate. About the water toll, nobody
who will listen. About the state of this sheet, the Guild, and join the queue.
