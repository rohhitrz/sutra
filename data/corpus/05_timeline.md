# Chronology of the Meridian Line

Dates are given as month and year of the Concord Era. The eight months of the year, in order, are
**Hollow, Thaw, Greening, Longlight, Dustfall, Emberwane, Frostmark, Deepwinter**. Day-of-month is
not kept in Line records.

---

## The older record

These entries are kept in the Syndicate's roll at Halvern Reach and are included here because
questions about the Line's institutions are asked more often than questions about its traffic.

- **Hollow 1012** — the Concord is sworn at Halvern Reach. All Line records begin here; none is older.
- **Greening 1041** — the first Tide Clock is built at Halvern Reach. It has three dials.
- **Dustfall 1047** — the Tide Clock is rebuilt with **four** dials. The fourth is set behind a
  brass plate at the time of building. The builder's instructions, if there were any, are lost.
- **Longlight 1053** — the **Waystone Tithe** is set by the Harbour Syndicate at two tenth-marks per
  wagon for each waystone passed, counting the first and the last, levied the whole length of the
  Line. The schedule then stands unaltered for ninety-one years.
- **Frostmark 1087** — the **Lamplighters' Guild** is chartered, paid by both terminus cities, with
  the sole right to declare a closed route open by relighting its fires.
- **Emberwane 1098** — the lake rises over the town of **Old Mirrow**. Mirrowmere takes its present
  shape. Nineteen of the town's people are not accounted for.
- **Thaw 1112** — the merchant council of Kesh Ithar is fixed at **nine seats**, held for life, one
  vote each, and is from that year called the Nine Tables.
- **Deepwinter 1126** — the water at Mirrowmere drops far enough for the roofs of Old Mirrow to be
  walked on. This is the ordinary Deepwinter drop and is not remarked on again until 1144, when it
  happens out of season.

## Before Book One

- **Thaw 1119** — Sera Volant is born at Varro's Ford.
- **Emberwane 1131** — the Vessel River floods at Varro's Ford. Ravenna Volant, cartographer, dies
  in the flood. Sera Volant is twelve.
- **Frostmark 1139** — a rockfall above the narrows closes the **Thennic Spur**. The Lamplighters'
  Guild lets the signal fires at the fourteenth and twenty-second waystones go out.
- **Greening 1141** — Sera Volant joins the caravan *Long Patience* as outrider.

## Book One — 1143

- **Hollow 1143** — the *Long Patience* leaves Halvern Reach with **fourteen wagons**, bound for
  Kesh Ithar, under master Oren Dask. Pell Varrow travels as an apprentice courier.
- **Hollow 1143** — the caravan crosses the Vessel River at the ninth waystone, at Varro's Ford.
- **Dustfall 1143** — the caravan reaches **Ossuary Gate**, the seventeenth waystone. Hesh ties
  fourteen knots in the season's cord.
- **Emberwane 1143** — the Ashen Compact closes the cisterns from the eighteenth to the twenty-second
  waystone and sets a water toll. The caravan pays at the eighteenth, nineteenth and twentieth.
- **Emberwane 1143** — the **standoff at the twenty-first waystone**: six days. Four of the Compact's
  people and two of the caravan's are hurt; the drover Attick Sell dies of his injuries on the fifth
  day. Sera Volant settles the toll on the sixth.
- **Frostmark 1143** — the *Long Patience* reaches Kesh Ithar. Tulvo Brann buys its glass and salt.
  **Book One ends.** The Thennic Spur is still closed.

## Book Two — 1144

- **Hollow 1144** — **Oren Dask retires.** The licence of the *Long Patience* passes to **Sera
  Volant**, who becomes its caravan master.
- **Greening 1144** — the **Lamplighters' Guild reopens the Thennic Spur**, relighting the fires at
  the fourteenth and twenty-second waystones. Nine caravans take the Spur in its first season.
- **Longlight 1144** — the Harbour Syndicate and the Nine Tables sign the **Kesh Ithar Accord**. The
  Waystone Tithe is cut to one tenth-mark per wagon per waystone and confined to waystones one to
  sixteen. The old schedule applies to every passage up to and including this month.
- **Dustfall 1144** — the water at Mirrowmere drops out of season. At Old Mirrow, **Marrowen Vehl
  tells Sera Volant that Ravenna Volant was his sister**, making him her uncle.
- **Dustfall 1144** — **Ilsa Mirren declares in writing** that she has been a member of the Quiet
  Ledger for nineteen years. She is not prosecuted.
- **Frostmark 1144** — **Ilsa Mirren stands down** as Keeper of the Tide Clock after twelve years.
  **Pell Varrow is appointed Keeper**, aged seventeen.
- **Frostmark 1144** — the Lamplighters' Guild establishes a **permanent station of nine** at
  Ossuary Gate. Hesh ties a double knot in the season's cord.

## Standing seasonal notes

These are not events. They are kept at the end of the chronology because caravan masters read it
for them.

- The **Ashen Flats** lie between the twelfth and twenty-fourth waystones. There is no water in
  them that is not carried in or paid for.
- The water at **Mirrowmere** drops far enough to walk the roofs of Old Mirrow in **Deepwinter**,
  in most years. Out-of-season drops are rare; the chronology records 1144.
- The **Vessel River** at Varro's Ford is crossable by loaded wagon in an ordinary season. It is
  not crossable in roughly four months of the eight, and which four varies.
- The Lamplighters keep a fire-pan at every one of the forty waystones. A route whose fires are out
  is a route that is shut.

## Not dated in this chronology

**The setting of the forty waystones.** No record of the Line is older than the Concord of 1012, and
this chronology begins where the records do. Who set the stones is not known, and the compiler
declines to repeat the several traditions about it, none of which is evidence.
