# Registry of Persons of the Meridian Line

Compiled at Halvern Reach. One entry per person. The registry records **office, origin and dates**
and does not record affiliations that the person has not declared to a Syndic's clerk — a limitation
the compiler notes here because it has been complained of.

Entries are alphabetical by family name where one is recorded. Persons for whom no family name is
recorded are entered under their given name, in place, and are **not** given an invented one; there
is one such entry in this registry. Ages are given as at the date stated and are not updated
silently.

---

## Volant, Sera

- **Office:** outrider of the caravan *Long Patience*, Greening 1141 – Hollow 1144; **caravan master
  of the *Long Patience*** from Hollow 1144
- **Born:** Thaw 1119, at Varro's Ford
- **Age:** 24 at the opening of the Hollow 1143 passage
- **Parent recorded:** Ravenna Volant (mother), deceased Emberwane 1131
- **Carries:** a brass astrolabe, inherited, limb worn, alidade cracked
- **Notes:** licensed master at twenty-four. No master has been licensed younger in the Concord's records.

## Volant, Ravenna

- **Office:** cartographer, unattached
- **Died:** Emberwane 1131, in the flood of the Vessel River at Varro's Ford
- **Surviving issue recorded:** Sera Volant
- **Notes:** her surveys of the Vessel crossing and of Old Mirrow were not recovered at the time of
  her death. Sibling recorded at Varro's Ford; see the entry for Vehl, Marrowen.

## Dask, Oren

- **Office:** caravan master of the *Long Patience* until Hollow 1144; retired Hollow 1144
- **Born:** 1085. Age 58 in 1143
- **Earlier office:** clerk, Harbour Syndicate tithe office, Halvern Reach
- **Notes:** twenty-two passages of the Line. Author of parts of the tithe schedule he spent his
  second career paying.

## Mirren, Ilsa

- **Office:** Keeper of the Tide Clock, Halvern Reach, 1132 – Frostmark 1144
- **Training:** cartographer
- **Notes:** holder of the Reach's only complete waystone survey. Declared affiliation to a Syndic's
  clerk in Dustfall 1144; the declaration is recorded in the Syndic's roll and not in this registry.

## Varrow, Pell

- **Office:** courier of Halvern Reach, to 1144; **Keeper of the Tide Clock** from Frostmark 1144
- **Age:** 16 in 1143
- **Apprenticed:** to Sera Volant for the Hollow 1143 passage, at the Syndic's expense
- **Notes:** youngest Keeper appointed.

## Brann, Tulvo

- **Office:** **fourth seat of the Nine Tables**, Kesh Ithar
- **Trade:** glass, salt, brass, carriage
- **Notes:** keeps the most complete private ledger of Line traffic held in either terminus city.
  Did most of the drafting of the Kesh Ithar Accord, Longlight 1144.

## Hesh

- **Office:** waystone-tender, Ossuary Gate (waystone 17)
- **Notes:** mute from birth. Keeps the Gate's records in knotted cord, one yard to the season,
  thirty-one seasons held and never found wrong. **No family name is recorded for this person**,
  and none has been offered; the registry does not invent one.

## Vehl, Marrowen

- **Office:** leader of the Ashen Compact
- **Called:** the Cartwright
- **Born:** at Varro's Ford. Went into the Ashen Flats at nineteen
- **Age:** about 50 in 1143
- **Notes:** sibling to Ravenna Volant, declared at Mirrowmere in Dustfall 1144 and entered here on
  that declaration. The registry had carried no family for this person for thirty years.

## Oye, Halbrecht

- **Office:** Harbour Syndic of Halvern Reach
- **Notes:** signatory for the Reach to the Kesh Ithar Accord, Longlight 1144. Appointed Pell Varrow
  Keeper of the Tide Clock, Frostmark 1144.

## Sell, Attick

- **Office:** drover, *Long Patience*, nine years
- **Died:** Emberwane 1143, of injuries taken at the twenty-first waystone

## On what this registry does not hold

The registry records a person's **declared** affiliations only. Membership of the Quiet Ledger is
therefore absent from every entry above, including the two entries to which it applies, and must be
sought in the Syndic's roll or in the narrative record. The compiler has argued for the change twice
and been refused twice.

The registry likewise records **one parent** where only one is recorded. Where a second parent is
not entered, nothing should be read into the omission except that no clerk entered it.
